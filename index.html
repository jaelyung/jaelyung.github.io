Quick answers

About Pocket Word

What is JLPT Pocket Word?

JLPT Pocket Word is an iPhone vocabulary app for Japanese learners. It organizes words by JLPT level from N5 to N1 and offers quizzes, review, pronunciation, and examples.

How long is a study session?

Each study set has 10 questions, so you can practice JLPT vocabulary in a short break.

How can I review words I missed?

Missed words return automatically for review. You can also review them after a quiz, save favorites, or find words in the vocabulary list.

Is there an app for Chinese vocabulary?

Yes. HSK Pocket Word is the companion app for Chinese HSK vocabulary practice.

Who makes Pocket Word?

Pocket Word apps are built and operated by Pocketword, a language-learning app company. For support or business inquiries, email pocketword.cs@pocketword.site.
